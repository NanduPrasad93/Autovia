About AutoVia

Redefining the Automobile Ownership & Care Lifecycle

AutoVia was built to eliminate the opacity, fragmentation, and uncertainty that vehicle owners face when seeking maintenance, repairs, or spare parts.

Modern automobiles are advanced technological assets, yet managing their upkeep has remained an analogue, fragmented process. AutoVia combines cloud software, verified supplier networks, and real-time maintenance analytics to provide vehicle owners, independent mechanics, specialized workshops, and part suppliers with an integrated digital ecosystem.

From your daily commute to long-distance journeys, AutoVia ensures that expert mechanics are always within reach, transparent quotes precede every repair, and full digital vehicle histories are permanently preserved.

Trusted Automotive Network

Standardized Integrity & Quality
  • Rigorous Verification Every mechanic and workshop is validated against state licensing, facilities, and technical capabilities.
  • Cost Transparency Protocol Upfront, unalterable digital repair estimates with separated labor, spare parts, and incidental costs.
  • Permanent Digital Passports Immutable records of all services and odometer readings that bolster resale value and maintenance hygiene.

Our Mission

To empower automobile owners with complete transparency, convenience, and control over their vehicles, while providing verified mechanics, accredited workshops, and genuine parts suppliers with a premier platform to deliver trusted automotive care.

  • Eradicate hidden fees and unverified repair charges.
  • Streamline emergency roadside mechanic dispatch.
  • Provide verified OEM part tracing and verification.

Our Vision

To become the global benchmark in intelligent automobile management—a world where every automobile has an intelligent digital record, every repair is honest and predictable, and sustainable transition towards electric mobility is seamless.

  • Unified fleet intelligence for ICE, Hybrid, and EV models.
  • Predictive maintenance algorithms saving billions in breakdowns.
  • An interconnected community of automotive craftsmanship.
